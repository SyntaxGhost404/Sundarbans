## Places (non-paranormal, reusable)

- Meals in Ashraful mama's room on the floor; sofa for adda; boro mamani brings milk at night.
- Escape route at night: climb down the one-storey roof using the window grill of Mama's house; main gate = caught.
- Nanur bari pond with ghat steps; only Oli swims (Fakhru + Moin cannot swim).
- Behind the pond: bamboo grove + another pond + narrow dead-end path; direct path blocked by bathrooms beside the pond → go round behind the mosque. Chalta tree behind the pond (they "steal" chalta; mamani mixes with salt-chili).
- Mamani's kitchen; open ground in front = cricket pitch; behind kitchen: vegetable patch, trees, cool breeze; a tree oozing RED SAP that Moin mistook for blood (Oli: "এইটা গাছের কষ").
- Mazar 10 sec from house; "mazarer shamner uthan" courtyard; darbar kitchen food: দরবারের খাসির মাংস, ডালের ঘাঁটি, দরবারের খিচুড়ি (for a zikir). Evening zikir in front of mazar. Urs anticipated (Nazib: "ওরশের সময় এটা খুব কাজে লাগবে" re drone).
- "পুরান বাড়ি" (old homestead) and its pond (elders: 200–300 yrs old) — jungle starts at its left bank.
- "নগিরা ছাড়া বাড়ি" jungle: abandoned homestead, a few centuries-old graves in the middle, big tree beside graveyard, an ancient banyan on the path; villagers use a shortcut through it at dawn to get to work; fields (ক্ষেত) beyond.
- Bazar: 10 min walk. Ancient tea stall, elderly owner "মোহন কাকু" with glasses; TV = Taheri waz in mornings, Bangla action films at night (the trio find this contrast funny); clay cups, milk tea, singara.
- Chapitala bridge: ~10 min walk from bazar, old bridge over a small river; sweet shop beside it sells rosmalai (৳350/kg, from deep freezer).
- Oli's childhood school (he lived at nanur bari as a child): school no longer exists; the road to it is broken, potholed, trees leaning over — eerie by day.
