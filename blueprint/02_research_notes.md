# Research notes (with sources)

## Raja Chapitala / Cumilla
- Union legend page (user screenshots; site down/cert error): ~600 AH a famed Raja lived in Chapitala; his palace "রংমহল" with family suddenly sank into the earth on the spot; cause unknown; village named "Raja Chapitala"; digging at the palace site still reveals red bricks; 1971 fierce battle, many killed, many muktijoddhas; famous pir Kazi Siraj Uddin Chishti (R.), annual Urs, ~1 lakh devotees, langar ("খানা") all year; Hindu–Muslim harmony. https://chapitalaup.comilla.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6990c78435ce18e1c07187d3
- 600 AH ≈ 1203–04 CE. Ranavankamalla Harikaladeva copperplate (Mainamati) dated 1220 CE names Pattikera as capital; Deva rulers ~13th c.; Harikela silver coins found in Mainamati hoards. https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Harikela_Coins , https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Mainamati
- Chapitala union: 23°38.3′N 90°56′E, ~15 km², pop ~23,100 (2017), postcode 3543; villages Chapitala (wards 5–9), Khapura (3), Pushkonirpar (4), Ulomoira (1–2); Wazifa Khatun High School; Farid Uddin Degree College. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapitala_Union
- Muradnagar on the Gumti; rivers Gumti, Buri, Old Titas; largest upazila of Cumilla; 22 unions; Chapitala under Bangora Bazar thana. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muradnagar_Upazila
- Banglapedia: Chapitala & Pantibazar = the two most significant 1971 clashes in Muradnagar; Companiganj Hat and Ramchandrapur Hat notable; crops paddy, wheat, mustard etc. https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Muradnagar_Upazila
- NEW UPAZILA: gazette 8 Jul 2026 split Muradnagar → "Bangra" upazila (10 unions incl. Chapitala; Bangra Purba = sadar). https://www.bssnews.net/bangla/news-flash/322230 ; https://www.ittefaq.com.bd/796520
- Illegal topsoil cutting: 26 Apr 2026 UNO + AC Land raid in Chapitala, Bangra Paschim, Purbadhair Paschim; 3 excavators removed, ৳1.3 lakh fines, a govt canal recovered (Deshrupantor). Khapura (Chapitala union) fined for soil cutting. Soil looted from temple land in Brahman Chapitala mouza (Rising BD, Sept 2025). Night excavators ("ভেকু"), batteries seized in raids elsewhere; cases under বালুমহাল ও মাটি ব্যবস্থাপনা আইন ২০১০ s.15(1). https://www.deshrupantor.com/683496 ; https://www.risingbd.com/amp/news/620354 ; https://dailyinqilab.com/index.php/bangladesh/news/867547
- Cumilla antiquity: "বালাগাজীর মুড়া" (Lalmai) ancient wall found during soil digging; Dept of Archaeology Cumilla regional office excavated. (TBS Bangla / Rising BD)
- Cumilla–Sylhet highway: Mainamati→Dharkhar 54 km; Companiganj, Debidwar big bazars (bdnews24, Mar 2026).

## Sundarbans — rules & seasons
- Closed to everyone 1 Jun–31 Aug; reopened 1 Sep 2026. Entry only with Forest Dept pass on designated routes; some areas need special permission; departures from Khulna, Mongla, Shyamnagar; 3-day/2-night standard; ~65 vessels from Khulna; DRONES FULLY BANNED; firearms banned for visitors; single-use plastic banned. https://www.prothomalo.com/lifestyle/travel/6zwubstf1k
- Tour ops: licensed vessel + armed forest guard; Karamjal & Harbaria day trips from Mongla; Katka (watchtower, grassland, beach walk), Kochikhali, Dublar Char, Hiron Point. https://tourguidebd.com/how-to-visit-sundarbans-from-dhaka/
- Crab harvesting banned 1 Jan–end Feb every year (so late Dec = rush). Golpata season Feb–Mar. https://bangla.thedailystar.net/environment/natural-resources/sundarban-forest/news-3903211
- Bonbibi puja at the start of Magh (mid-Jan); revered by Hindu & Muslim forest workers. Dakshin Rai = tiger-lord in lore.
- Tiger census 2024: 125 tigers (announced 8 Oct 2024; 21 cubs photographed). https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/361154/tiger-population-in-sundarbans-rises-to-125
- Tiger-death compensation ৳3 lakh if photo proof of the body (forest rules, 2022 report).
