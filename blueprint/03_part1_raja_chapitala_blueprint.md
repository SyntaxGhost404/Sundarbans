# Part 1 — "রংমহলের ইট" (The Bricks of Rongmohol)
### Raja Chapitala, Cumilla · 16–22 December 2026 · fully rational mystery

---

## 0. One-paragraph pitch

Fakhru is home for his US winter break. The trio go to nanur bari in Raja Chapitala and walk into a village gripped by the old legend: at night, people hear the ankle-bells (নূপুর) of the Raja's dancers from the field mound where his palace "Rongmohol" is said to have sunk into the earth around 600 Hijri. Red lights drift over the mound in the fog. Pits appear and refill overnight. A dog is found dead there without a wound. A brick-kiln owner wants to cut the mound for soil. Everything looks like the legend coming awake. The truth is all human, layered, and a little heartbreaking. A respected local businessman is looting real early-medieval antiquities from the mound, and the coins are worth crores. He hides the dig behind a staged haunting built from gadgets the trio unknowingly carried from Dhaka, and the dig is cutting into the unmarked graves of 1971 martyrs.

## 3. The legend, and the real history underneath it

- **Legend (union website):** around 600 AH a famous Raja lived here; his palace Rongmohol sank into the earth overnight with his whole family; digging at the site still brings up red bricks.
- **History:** 600 AH ≈ 1203–04 CE. In the Cumilla region this is the period of the **Pattikera** kingdom. The Ranavankamalla Harikaladeva copperplate of 1220 CE names Pattikera as capital, and silver **Harikela** coins turn up in Mainamati hoards. A local chief's brick house, mound or shrine from that period is exactly the kind of site that leaves "red bricks under the field".
- **Why a palace "sinks":** Choto mama and Fakhru settle on the rational coda. This is the Gumti and Buri floodplain. Floods lay silt year after year; the August 2024 flood alone left inches in a week. Eight hundred years of silt buries anything. "রংমহল এক রাতে ডোবেনি; নদী তাকে আটশো বছর ধরে একটু একটু করে মাটি চাপা দিয়েছে। কিংবদন্তি আটশো বছরকে এক রাতে গুঁজে দিয়েছে।"

## 4. The hidden truth (author's eyes only)

1. **Motive.** Salam's fish project was wiped out in the **August 2024 Cumilla flood**. He borrowed heavily to re-dig. In 2025 the re-dig at the mound's edge turned up terracotta plaque fragments and a pot of small silver coins (Harikela-type). A Dhaka "antique" buyer offered life-changing money for more.
2. **Method.** He cannot dig the mound openly. It is *khas* land, villagers revere it, and it hides something he doesn't yet know about. So he digs **from his pond side at night**, refills the test pits before dawn, and covers them with leaves.
3. **The haunting.** He commissions Riaz to build three "pond bird- and fish-thief deterrents" (বক/চোর তাড়ানোর যন্ত্র): a weatherproof box with a 12 V battery, a programmable timer, a small amplifier and speaker looping a downloaded *ghungroo* file, and red LED strobes. Riaz built them from **the parts Oli brought from Dhaka**. Salam moves the units into the jungle and the mound bushes, set for 1:30–2:30 am. In fog the red strobes become "রংমহলের বাতি" and the jingle becomes the dancers' নূপুর.
4. **The alibi.** On haunting nights Salam sits in the front row of the darbar mahfil until 3 am, seen by hundreds, including Ashraful mama. The timers are what make the alibi work. And on mahfil nights the whole village is at the darbar while the loudspeakers drown out the dig's small generator and pump.
5. **The dead dog.** Kala Mia ran an illegal hook-line (অবৈধ সংযোগ) from a field irrigation pump's line to power a dewatering pump in the deep pit. The water table is low in December, which is exactly why they dig now. Kashem's dog touched the wire: singed paws, "no wound". Superstition says "রাজার প্রহরী মেরেছে".
6. **The martyrs.** The north end of the mound is where villagers secretly buried freedom fighters killed in the 1971 Chapitala clash; Banglapedia lists it as one of the upazila's two biggest. Hasmat keeps vigil there with a lantern every December night. The looters' deepest pit is cutting into those graves. Bones are being thrown into the bushes.
7. **The exit route.** Salam's sweet van leaves before dawn with thermocol boxes of rosmalai pots marked "ঢাকা অর্ডার". On extraction nights some boxes carry coins and terracotta under the ice and rosmalai. Cumilla rosmalai going to Dhaka is the most innocent cargo in Bangladesh.
8. **Bhuiyan** really is cutting topsoil illegally elsewhere (the April 2026 raid in Chapitala is real background), and he wants the mound. He is a crook, but not this crook.

## 6. Investigation, beat by beat

**How amateurs move.** Everyone in the village knows them as "আশরাফুল সাহেবের ভাগ্নে". Gossip reaches the house within an hour, so every outing needs a cover story: "Fakhru's US friends want drone shots", fishing, Oli's old school road, buying rosmalai, the evening bazar tea. They search nothing they don't have a right to enter. Their only advantages are attention, patience, local relationships and one trustworthy adult.

### Chapter-style beats
1. **Dhaka, 12–15 Dec.** Fakhru's return. Riaz's request. Oli and Moin buy timer relays, amplifier boards, red LED strips and waterproof boxes "for Riaz's pond project". *(Plant #1, played as colour.)*
2. **Victory Day journey.** Sayedabad bus; fog after Daudkandi; Companiganj; fruit for the mamas; CNG; the banyan, the mosque, the mazar, then nanur bari. Mamani's feast. Ashraful mama's new rule. Oli hands Riaz the packet in the courtyard *(plant #2, one line)*. Roof room, tin roof, pigeons. At 1:40 am Moin hears faint jingling from beyond the jungle.
3. **Tea-stall morning.** At Mohon kaku's: nupur, red lights, Kashem's dead dog, "লাল চাদরের রাজা". Bhuiyan drives up, mocks "ভূতের গল্প", lets slip that he has "কাগজ" (papers) for the mound. Salam arrives and rebukes him eloquently on "রাজার স্মৃতি", and the room applauds. The reader likes Salam.
4. **Choto mama.** He catches their excitement and makes them promise caution. He tells the legend straight, converts 600 AH to around 1203 CE, and names Pattikera. On jinn: "জ্বিন আছে, কোরআনে আছে। কিন্তু মানুষের কারসাজিকে জ্বিনের নামে চালানো দুনিয়ার সবচেয়ে পুরনো ব্যবসা।"
5. **The mound by daylight** (drone-shot cover). Red brick bits, a terracotta fragment, the "blood" that turns out to be sap. Dawn drone footage from the roof shows refilled rectangles and a path toward Salam's pond, not toward Bhuiyan's road. Salam waves from his pond bank: "ভাইগ্না, ড্রোনে আমার পুকুরটাও তুইলা দিও!"
6. **Near miss.** They're late for Maghrib. Mamani's scolding; Ashraful mama's silence is worse. They resolve to stay out of sight. *(Secrecy holds.)*
7. **Jumu'ah, then Hasmat.** Choto mama introduces them. Hasmat is hostile, then tells the 1971 story and the hidden graves at the north end. He has seen "men from the pond side" and a light that "isn't my lantern"; nobody listened when he told the union member.
8. **Trail cam.** Faisal straps Fakhru's camera to a jarul tree facing the north end.
9. **Night escape** via the roof and Mama's window grill. At the banyan the jingle starts at exactly 1:30. They trace it to a box lashed inside a hollow. Inside: the timer relay, an amplifier board with a **Patuatuli shop sticker ("১৩৫০/-")** and an SD card. Oli goes white: "এইগুলা তো আমি কিনছি।" *(Mid-story gut punch.)*
10. **Footage.** 11:10 pm: Hasmat praying, and the reader's suspicion flips to him. 2:14 am: a muffled figure with a red headlamp, a spade and a **thermocol box**, walking from the pond side with a slight limp.
11. **Riaz on the roof** (gentle confrontation). He's stricken. He shows the WhatsApp order from "সালাম কাকা — মাছের প্রজেক্ট, ৩টা মেশিন". Riaz becomes their inside man: he will "check on the machines" at the pond and finds two missing.
12. **The alibi problem.** Salam was at the mahfil all night. Fakhru's notebook: "ভূত সময় মেনে চলে, মানুষ সময় সেট করে" (ghosts keep time; people set timers). The alibi was manufactured.
13. **Sweet-shop recon.** Moin and Oli go to buy rosmalai "for Dhaka". The old-draft line returns: "আজকে মাত্র তিন কেজি আছে" — while a stack of sealed thermocol boxes marked "ঢাকা অর্ডার" sits behind. Salam is charming and gives them extra. *(Doubt both ways.)*
14. **Red herring escalates.** Bhuiyan's excavator rolls up to the mound at dusk. Villagers led by Salam block it. Bhuiyan: "ভিটার মাটি আমি কাটমুই।" The reader thinks: Bhuiyan is the ghost-maker, scaring people off so he can cut it. Fakhru: "কিন্তু পায়ের দাগ পুকুরের দিক থেকে।"
15. **Setback.** The trail cam is stolen that night. Moin's habit of copying footage every morning saves the evidence. Mohon kaku later mentions Kala Mia asking about "গাছে বান্ধা বাক্সটা".
16. **The coin.** On a "fishing" pretext at the fish project's pump outlet, Faisal finds a small silver coin in the outflow mud. Choto mama: "আরবি না… অন্য লিপি।" A phone search leads to Harikela coinage (7th–13th c.), which fits the legend's era. The motive crystallises.
17. **The bone.** Hasmat brings them, trembling, a human jaw fragment he found thrown in the bushes near the north-end pit. The case stops being a game.
18. **Prediction.** The final mahfil night (Sun 20th) is extraction night: loudspeakers and an empty village; Bhuiyan's excavator threatens the mound, so Salam must finish first; Salam told Riaz to "collect the machines Monday"; and the trio see a Monday "ঢাকা অর্ডার" stack twice the usual size.
19. **Plan with Choto mama.** He insists on the police and the trio agree. He'll phone the Bangra thana OC (he knows him from mahfil arrangements) on Moin's signal. No confrontation.
20. **Climax, after moonset (about 2 am).** Moin and Fakhru watch from the roof with binoculars; Oli and Faisal hide near the bridge; Riaz keeps watch on the pond. Hasmat has to be physically held back when the diggers lift a large clay pot and terracotta plaques from the north end. At 4 am the van leaves; Moin signals; Mama calls. The van is stopped at the highway checkpoint toward Companiganj. Under the rosmalai and ice: the coin hoard, plaques, and a sack of bones the crew meant to dump elsewhere.

## 7. The reveal and the twist ladder

**Gathering** after Fajr on Mon 21 Dec, at the union parishad or the darbar courtyard: UNO, OC, villagers, Ashraful mama (finding out everything at once), Hasmat, Riaz. **Fakhru explains**, Feluda-style, taking the §5 table point by point.

Twist ladder (each rung breaks the reader's previous theory):
1. *It's the legend.* No: every phenomenon has a mechanism.
2. *It's Bhuiyan, scaring people off his soil.* No: the trail leads from the pond side, and Bhuiyan is guilty only of soil theft elsewhere (the mobile court fines him separately).
3. *It's the shawl-wrapped old man.* No: Hasmat is guarding martyrs.
4. *It's Riaz; he built the machines.* No: he was duped, using parts **Oli himself carried**.
5. **It's Salam, the heritage champion with the perfect mahfil alibi.** His sweet van is the exit route, and his flood debt is the motive.
6. **Optional family twist:** Nazib quietly admits he saw their muddy shoes and the 4 am battery charging, and knew they were climbing down the grill. He didn't tell his father because he was secretly photographing Bhuiyan's night trucks for a complaint to the new UNO. One of his photos, timestamped, shows Salam's van at the mound road at 4:05 am on an earlier "ঢাকা অর্ডার" day. *(Drop this rung if you want Nazib fully in the dark.)*

**Aftermath**
- Ashraful mama: fury ("তোমরা রাইতে ছাদ বাইয়া নামতা?!"), then a long silence, then he phones their parents to *praise* them. The threat that forced the secrecy turns into pride.
- Mamani cries and makes pitha.
- Choto mama: "মামুরা, এইবার তো আর থামাইতে পারলাম না।"
- The UNO promises a memorial for the martyrs and informs the Dept of Archaeology; the mound goes under protection.
- **Seed for Part 2:** at the mahfil, Moin noticed shutki sacks gifted to the darbar, stencilled "গাজী ফিশারিজ · জয়মনি, মোংলা". A Haji saheb from the Sundarbans. They never met him.

## 8. Guardrails used

- **Tech:** the drone flies twice (dawn recon, soil-heap shot); the trail cam records twice and is then stolen. Neither solves anything. Deductions come from timing, soil colour, the moon, a price sticker, a WhatsApp order, and testimony.
- **Choto mama:** context, introductions, Islamic counsel, and one phone call made on *their* evidence. No secret knowledge, no rescue.
- **Respect:** the mazar, the darbar and the mahfil are moral anchors, never the source of crime. Criminals exploit the crowd; the darbar is not involved. Real people are not named; the 1971 battle details beyond "a major Chapitala clash" are fictionalised.
- **Old draft reused, de-paranormalised:** the roof room and grill escape, the jungle graves and banyan, Mohon kaku's TV contrast, rosmalai "only 3 kg left", Riaz's electrical parts, the red sap, the shawl-wrapped old man, the bone in the old draft's pond turned into a martyr's bone.
