# Part 2 — "দোয়ানির ডাক" (The Call of the Doyani)
### The Sundarbans · 19–26 December 2027 · human intrigue fully solved, two linked phenomena left unexplained

---

## 0. One-paragraph pitch

A year after Raja Chapitala, the trio, now confident that every "haunting" has a human behind it, travel to the Sundarbans as guests of Haji Abdur Rob Gazi. He is a wealthy Mongla fisherman, a murid of their great-grandfather's darbar, and, as it turns out, Dr. Nasir's patient and the owner of the Cox's Bazar shutki shop Nasir has bought from for years. Nasir, who "couldn't possibly come", is already on the boat eating bhetki. Haji saheb is carrying a wound: two months ago his boatman Kader was "taken by a tiger" at a remote confluence the fishermen call **পরীর দোয়ানি** (the Fairies' Doyani). The body was never found and the young widow is now shunned as a "tiger widow". At the doyani, a voice calls Moin's name in Nasir's voice while Nasir sleeps beside him, and pale lights walk over the water. Most of it turns out to be human. The tiger story is a lie. Haji saheb's nephew runs a poison-fishing and illegal-shutki racket under his uncle's good name, protected by a pirate gang. Kader is **alive**, held as leverage. But two things at the doyani are never explained by anyone, and the story leaves them that way.

Crab harvesting closes on 1 Jan, so the forest is crowded with crab boats in the last week of December, which is perfect cover for criminals.

## 4. The hidden truth (author's eyes only)

1. **The racket.** Since Haji's 2026 surgery, Shahin has run the Dublar depot. He finances poison-fishing (pesticide in small sanctuary khals at ebb) and dries the catch on a hidden platform in the forest. The reddish shutki sells higher and is blended into the depot's legitimate stock under the respected "গাজী" name, going to wholesalers and to Tanvir's Cox's Bazar shop. Deer meat from snare lines is a side line. Shahin pays the **জোনাক বাহিনী** monthly and feeds them tips on other mohajons' boats. That is why Haji's boats are "never attacked", which Haji gratefully credits to the darbar's dua.
2. **Kader found the platform** while crab-fishing and told Shahin he would go to Haji after the gon.
3. **29 Oct, dusk.** Shahin, Bachchu and two pirates seized Kader at the doyani. Bachchu slashed Kader's vest with a dao and smeared it with deer blood. Shahin announced "বাঘে নিছে" with Bachchu as eyewitness: "ভাটার সময় চরে কাঁকড়া ধরতেছিল, চাঁদের আলোয় স্পষ্ট দেখছি বাঘ টাইনা নিল।" The forest department paid nothing because there was no photo of a body. Haji paid the widow himself, out of guilt: he had pushed Kader to go to that khal that day, after the old skipper had refused ("জো'র দিন, সন্ধ্যায় দোয়ানির চর ডুবা").
4. **Shahin couldn't bring himself to order a murder.** Jonak kept Kader alive as leverage over Shahin, chained at night and forced to dry the poisoned fish and to row the supply dinghy.
5. **Night 1, 19 Dec.** On a supply run near Joymoni, Kader heard voices from the ghat and whistled his signature tune. Jamal heard it.
6. **Night 3, 21–22 Dec.** At 03:10 the pirates' dinghy passed the doyani with Kader rowing, red gamchha on his head, oar on the **left**. At dawn they walked the snare line, which left Kader's **four-toed left footprint** pressed in after the night tide.
7. **The guests found the platform**, so Shahin told Jonak by button phone to break the platform and **move Kader** to another khal (overheard by Oli at Dublar Char on 24 Dec).
8. **25 Dec, dawn.** The pirates were moving Kader through fog just as Oli and Jamal drifted into the same side khal on a failing dinghy engine.

## 6. The two unexplained phenomena (never solved)

They are linked by **place** (the doyani and the riverbank behind Rahela's hut), **time** (the darkest nights, around 2 am), **behaviour** (they imitate the beloved; they fade at the adhan or the prophetic dua), and **lore** (the skipper's "ওনারা" of the gewa grove, the old tiger-graves).

### A. The Calling (নাম ধরে ডাক)
- **Night 3, about 01:40:** "বাবু… এই বাবু… এদিকে আয়" from the dark forest, in **Nasir's exact voice** and with the family pet name only Nasir and Fakhru use, while Nasir sleeps a foot away. Fakhru hears it too; Oli sleeps through. Moin half-rises; Fakhru grips his wrist. The skipper, awake at the wheel, whispers: "সাড়া দিবেন না।" Nasir wakes, recites **আঊযু বিকালিমাতিল্লাহিত তা-ম্মাতি মিন শাররি মা খালাক** (Sahih Muslim 2708) and Ayat al-Kursi. Silence.
- **31 Oct (told on 26 Dec):** Rahela heard **Kader's voice** call "রাহেলা" three times at about 2 am behind her hut. That night Kader lay chained and feverish, tens of kilometres away.
- **Night 7, about 01:55:** a woman's voice calls "কাদের… বাপ কাদের…". Kader, shaking: his **mother's voice; she died three years ago**.
- *Rational candidates mentioned, never confirmed:* half-sleep hallucination, grief, wind in the gewa snags. *Against them:* two awake listeners on night 3, a specific name and a specific voice, and no human anywhere.

### B. The Doyani Lights (দোয়ানির আলো)
- **Night 3:** pale bluish-white lights, five or six, drifting in a slow line low over the confluence **against the ebb and the wind**, circling toward the graves' bank. Rafiqul cocks his gun. They fade.
- **Night 7:** they return while the Coast Guard boat lies at anchor 200 m away and every criminal is in custody or has fled miles inland. The Coast Guard searchlight finds empty water. Nasir climbs to the wheelhouse roof and gives the **adhan** (the Sahih hadith that Shaytan flees at the adhan). The lights go out one by one.
- **Trail cam:** on night 3 the camera recorded the 03:10 dinghy but **nothing** at the time of the lights. On night 7 it fired **37 times between 01:52 and 02:09**, and every frame is empty water and trees. At 02:03:41 the audio has a sound that might be a word or might be a night heron.
- *Rational candidates mentioned, never confirmed:* marsh gas (আলেয়া), bioluminescence, reflections. *Against them:* movement against the current, witnesses including the Coast Guard, and both times they ended at the adhan.

**Writing rule:** never confirm, never debunk. Nasir: "যা ব্যাখ্যা করা যায়, তা শেষ বিন্দু পর্যন্ত করব। যা যায় না, তার সামনে মাথা নিচু করব।" Ahsanul mama: jinn are real (Qur'an 55:15, Surah al-Jinn; 7:27, they see us where we don't see them), but most "ghosts" are people. Seek refuge in Allah, don't answer the call, don't be afraid.

12. **Fog (25 Dec, dawn).** Oli and Jamal go in the motor-dinghy to fetch the re-mounted trail cam, 150 m away, no landing. The fog closes. They vanish. Whistles and shouts. The skipper: "ভুলোয় ধরছে।" Nasir takes command. When the fog thins: "মঈন, ড্রোন।" Moin already has it ready (Oli's rule: always one minute from the air).
13. **The forbidden sky.** About 09:10, no ceremony and no permission: Moin is airborne within a minute and Rafiqul says nothing. Moin flies (Oli, the real pilot, is the missing one). The tied dinghy, the smoke, the camp, a chained man in a red gamchha, and two figures hiding behind a fallen sundari. Walkie-talkie. The Coast Guard boat appears in the main channel (the Kotka protocol). The officer: "ড্রোন? সুন্দরবনে?", then "ড্রোনের কথা আজ আমি কিছু শুনি নাই", and he asks Moin to fly again.
14. **Kader.** Moin guides from the deck via drone and walkie-talkie. Oli cuts the pirates' boat loose so they can't take Kader away; the pirates bolt into the forest; one is caught in the mud; Rafiqul's warning shots; the Coast Guard reaches the camp. Kader is freed. Nasir tends to chain sores and dehydration. Kader's story.
15. **The truth's resting place.** Haji's grief; he phones the Coast Guard himself to stop Shahin's trawler (caught near the sea mouth). Bachchu taken at the depot. Rafiqul's phone: a daughter, Banani. At Kotka that evening, **Nasir explains**, clue by clue (§5).
16. **Beyond explanation.** Night 7 at the doyani with the Coast Guard alongside: the lights, the mother's voice, the adhan. The trail cam's 37 empty frames. 26 Dec: Joymoni at dusk, Kader and Rahela; Rahela's October night. Mama's last call ("দোয়া কাজ করে, মামুরা… কিন্তু চোরের চাঁদাকে দোয়া ভাবলে সেটা আর দোয়া থাকে না।"). Haji's vow; the Cox's Bazar shop pulls the reddish stock. No report mentions the drone; Oli names it "উদ্ধারকারী". 27 Dec: Kader to hospital in Khulna. 28 Dec: Jahanabad 06:00 in a whole cabin, Fakhru's 34th tick at Narail, "পরীর দোয়ানি — টিক দেওয়া যাবে না". 9 Jan: Fakhru flies. Moin's last line.

## 8. Plausibility guardrails

- **Amateurs, not officials:** they search nothing without the guard, arrest nobody, and hand every piece of evidence to the forest department and Coast Guard. Their power is attention: a whistle, a footprint, a calendar box, a smell, a logbook line.
- **Tech:** the trail cam gives one decisive clip (and one ambiguous record); offline GPS gives coordinates; the drone flies twice, against the ban and without ceremony (Oli's scouting flight, Moin's fog search), and only ever shows where to look: people do the rest; walkie-talkies are ordinary fishing gear. No signal most of the time.
- **Ahsanul mama:** three calls (setup; Kochikhali counsel plus one remembered remark; closing). Never a solution.
- **Nasir as leader:** chooses the route risks, says "ওড়াও" when the drone is the right tool and keeps it quiet afterwards, reads the vest and the shutki like a surgeon, protects Haji's dignity, keeps faith and reason in one hand.
- **Travel realism:** only real Bangladesh Railway services with checked times, routes and off days (Sundarban Express 726 via Rajbari–Kushtia, off Wednesday; Jahanabad Express 825/826 via Narail, off Monday), always one whole AC-berth cabin.
- **Respect:** Bonbibi lore is shown as living folk culture; the protagonists answer with prophetic duas, not mockery. The pirate gang and all criminals are fictional. Forest staff and the Coast Guard are depicted as stretched but honest.
